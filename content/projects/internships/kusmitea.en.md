## Context

During my first year of engineering school, I completed a manual labor internship at Kusmi Tea, a company specializing in the import/export and sale of tea. I worked on the production line and in IT support.

## Designing a production tracking tool

To minimize tea bag counting errors, I designed a production tracking tool using an industrial programmable logic controller and the LADDER language. This tool counted the number of bags produced in real time and grouped them into batches on the production line conveyors before boxing.

## Contributing to the rollout of new payment terminals

During my internship, the company was upgrading all its electronic payment terminals across every store in France. I contributed to configuring them and verifying they were working correctly.
